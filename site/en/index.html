Good to know

Do you deliver?

No. You order by phone on 020 365 7971 and pick up your food from us. There is no online ordering either.

Can I book a table?

No, reservations are not possible.

How soon is my order ready?

We make everything fresh and it is ready fast. When you call, we agree on a pick-up time together.

Is the food halal?

All the chicken we use is halal. That does not apply to the whole menu: it also has pork, beef, duck and shrimp.

I have an allergy. What should I know?

Always tell us when you call. This site does not yet have a checked allergen list per dish, but the kitchen can tell you what is in a dish, its sauce and its broth. Add-ons can contain allergens too. Cross-contamination can happen, so we cannot guarantee that a dish is free from any allergen.

What is vegetarian?

No. 6, 13, 37, 39, 40 and 46 are vegetarian according to our menu. Vegetarian is not automatically vegan: 39 and 40 contain egg, for example.

What do the peppers mean?

The number of peppers comes from our printed menu. One pepper is spicy, two peppers is extra spicy.

Are you open when there is a football match?

On Sundays with a football match we open at 12:00. The other times are listed under opening hours.

How do I pay?

You pay when you pick up.
